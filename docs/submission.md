**Category:** Builder → Intelligent Contracts

**Title:** DeltaBisect: Consensus Merkle disagreement game

**Notes / Description:**

DeltaBisect is a standalone two-party dispute primitive for batches of documentary classifications. Parties commit domain-bound Merkle roots; authenticated child openings isolate the first disagreement in at most three rounds. GenLayer leader and validators independently fetch the disputed full report, verify its pinned SHA-256, classify the fixed question and audit a material quotation. Exact YES/NO/UNCERTAIN agreement can disprove one or both committed vectors; other leaves remain unverified. Shared deadlines produce procedural forfeits, while equal roots explicitly terminate as MATCHED_UNVERIFIED. Four gasless StudioNet deployments cover a corrected announcement, an explicit negative, unresolved contradiction and matching roots. All 32 receipts finalized with MAJORITY_AGREE, including three expected rejections. The repo includes 48 direct tests, 6,561 Merkle pair checks, source binding and fresh-state verification. Synthetic documents demonstrate the protocol, not real-world truth.
